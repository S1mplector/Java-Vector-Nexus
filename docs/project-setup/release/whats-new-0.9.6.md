# JVN 0.9.6

- The tutorial opens on Ginger's autumn watercolor with glass buttons and a music visualizer along the bottom edge.
- Settings, Save, and Load slide in over the background. The title artwork fills the window at different aspect ratios.
- The lo-fi track loops through the title screen, lessons, and saved games at the player's Music volume.
